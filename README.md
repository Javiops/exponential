## How I Work

- **Gets me pumped:** hard problems. I'd rather spend days or weeks on one hard problem than solve several trivial ones. Problem solving is what drives me; it's what keeps me up at night until I crack the challenge. When I'm on a task, I'm hyperfocused on it.
- **Disappoints me:** lack of ambition. Teams that aim for "good enough" when they could aim for great, and people who stop at the first answer that works instead of digging for the right one. I'd rather be around people who set the bar uncomfortably high and push me to reach it.
- **Strengths:** algorithmic thinking, great problem solving, deep mathematical knowledge, very competitive.
- **Improvement points:** until recently I was focused on solving puzzles rather than problems. Olympiads trained me to crack well-defined challenges, and until this summer I neglected my potential to create rather than to solve. Docplanner, Videns and Battlecode are me changing that: now I'm learning to pick the problem worth solving, not just the solution.
- **Known behaviours:** heads-down and hyperfocused on one hard problem at a time; I'll surface with a result, not a status update. Direct and concise, and I back claims with numbers.
